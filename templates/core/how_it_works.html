How BorrowHub BD works

BorrowHub sits between the owner and the renter. We run booking, payment, the security deposit and the return check, so neither side has to trust a stranger.

If you're renting

  1. Search and pick dates. Filter by city, price, delivery and availability.
  2. Send a request. The price breakdown shows rent, delivery fee and the refundable deposit.
  3. Pay after the owner accepts. Send money to BorrowHub's bKash, Nagad or Rocket number and enter your TrxID. You have {{ SITE.payment_window_hours }} hours.
  4. Collect the item. Pick it up or get it delivered. The owner marks the handover.
  5. Return it. After inspection you get your deposit back. If the owner reports damage, you can accept or dispute it.

If you're an owner

  1. List your item. Add photos, a daily price, a deposit and your rules.
  2. Review requests. Accept the ones that suit you. Dates are blocked automatically.
  3. Hand over once it's paid. You only hand over after BorrowHub confirms the payment.
  4. Inspect on return. Report damage within the inspection window, or release the deposit.
  5. Get paid. Your earnings, minus the {{ SITE.commission_percent|floatformat:"-1" }}% commission, go to your payout account.
