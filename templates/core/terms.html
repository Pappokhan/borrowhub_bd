Terms & deposit policy

This is a starter policy for your marketplace. Have a lawyer review it before launch.

1. The marketplace

{{ SITE.site_name }} connects owners who list items with renters who need them for a short time. We manage booking, payment collection, security deposits and payouts. The rental agreement is between the owner and the renter.

2. Fees

Renters pay the listed daily price for the booked days, plus a delivery fee if delivery is chosen, plus a refundable security deposit. Owners pay a commission of {{ SITE.commission_percent|floatformat:"-1" }}% of the rental amount, deducted when the rental completes.

3. Payment

Renters pay {{ SITE.site_name }} (not the owner) using bKash, Nagad or Rocket. Owners should hand over an item only after the booking shows Paid.

4. Security deposit

The deposit is held until the owner inspects the returned item. The owner may deduct the cost of damage up to the deposit amount, with a written note. The renter may accept or dispute within {{ SITE.inspection_window_hours }} hours; silence counts as acceptance.

5. Cancellations

Bookings can be cancelled before handover. Paid bookings are refunded in full. Requests not answered within 48 hours, and accepted bookings not paid within {{ SITE.payment_window_hours }} hours, expire automatically.

6. Prohibited items & conduct

No illegal items, weapons or hazardous goods. Members must give accurate descriptions and treat items with care. Accounts that break these rules can be suspended.

7. Disputes

If members can't agree, {{ SITE.site_name }} staff review the evidence and make a final decision on the deposit.
